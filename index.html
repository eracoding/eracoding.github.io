AI researcher & engineer

I study how machine learning models can recover changes over time from incomplete observations. I’m especially interested in longitudinal medical imaging and in combining images with clinical text to distinguish disease progression from changes in how the data were collected.

I’m completing a dual MSc at Télécom SudParis and the Asian Institute of Technology. My recent work spans reverse disease progression in brain MRI, multi-agent language models for low-resource languages, and real-time multimodal systems.

Selected publications

MICCAI · SASHIMI 2026

Reverse Spatio-Temporal Disease Progression Modelling

Ulugbek Shernazarov, Moucheng Xu, Inomjon Ramatov

We combine a 3D vector-quantized autoencoder with a Neural ODE to estimate earlier brain MRI states from later scans. We evaluate the method on longitudinal ADNI scans and controlled Morpho-MNIST sequences.

EMNLP · REALM 2026

Disentangling Topology and Diversity in Multi-Agent LLMs for Multilingual Low-Resource Emotion Detection

Ulugbek Shernazarov, Charitha Ruwansiri Weerakon Basnayake, Abdelkhaleq El Jarjini, Noel Crespi, Praboda Rajapaksha

Across nine languages and a fixed three-call budget, we compare parallel aggregation with sequential refinement and test how each interacts with sampling, role prompts, and learned QLoRA specialists.

NLTM 2026

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning for Medical Text Summarization: A Comparative Study of LoRA, Prompt Tuning, and Full Fine-Tuning

Ulugbek Shernazarov, Rostislav Svitsov, Bin Shi

We compare three ways to adapt Flan-T5 for PubMed summarization. LoRA achieved the highest ROUGE-1 score in our Flan-T5-Large experiments while updating only 0.6% of its parameters.
